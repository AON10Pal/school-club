Club Activities

• Mechanical Design and Assembly: Members sketch blueprints, model components using computer-aided design (CAD) software, and physically assemble the chassis and mechanisms of the robots. • Software Programming: Code enthusiasts write and debug scripts in languages like C++ or Python to give the robots functionality, enabling them to navigate autonomously and process sensor data. • Competitive Strategy and Testing: Teams run mock trials on custom tracks to rigorously test their robots, fine-tuning mechanisms and strategies to prepare for regional and national tournaments. • Community Outreach and Mentorship: Experienced students host interactive workshops for local middle schools and demonstrate their projects at science fairs to spark early interest in robotics.
